This policy is a general-purpose template describing how AR Tours collects and uses information through this website. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. 1. Who we are

AR Tours ("we", "us", "our") is a private tour operator based at 52 Spencer Street, Melbourne VIC 3000, Australia. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

2. Information we collect

When you use our booking form or contact us directly, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, and phone number
  • Your preferred tour, travel dates, and number of guests
  • Any special requests or messages you include
  • Basic technical information such as browser type and general location, collected automatically by standard web hosting logs

We do not collect payment card details through this website. Payment, where applicable, is arranged directly with our team by phone, email, or in person.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to booking requests and enquiries
  • To confirm tour arrangements, pricing, and pickup details
  • To communicate with you before, during, and after your tour
  • To improve our services and website

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

4. How your information is handled

Booking form submissions are sent via email (through the EmailJS service) directly to our team's inbox. We retain enquiry and booking information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, respond to your enquiry, and meet any legal or accounting obligations.

5. Third-party services

This site uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • EmailJS — to deliver booking form submissions by email
  • WhatsApp — if you choose to contact us via the WhatsApp button
  • GitHub Pages — our website hosting provider

6. Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser may store minimal technical data (such as page preferences) locally to make the site function correctly.

7. Your rights

You may ask us at any time what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, subject to any legal obligations we have to retain certain records. Contact us using the details below.

8. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults arranging travel, which may include children as guests on a booked tour. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children online.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
